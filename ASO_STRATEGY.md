## Differentiation

"The only prompt builder with a live articulated figure preview and 30+ decomposed categories — so every prompt element is mix-and-matchable."

Most tools are either prompt browsers (you copy someone else's work) or generators (the AI does everything). PosePrompter is the missing middle layer: a structured creative tool where you assemble professional-grade prompts from categorized building blocks, preview the pose visually, and share collections with a community marketplace.
